Pseudo-code: Simple Word Counter

1. Input paragraph text
2. Normalize text: lowercase, remove punctuation
3. Split text into words
4. Create empty Map<String, Integer>
5. For each word:
   a. If word exists in map, increment count
   b. Else, put word with count = 1
6. Print all words with frequency
7. Example run:
   Sports → 3
   bring → 1
   people → 1
   teamwork → 1
