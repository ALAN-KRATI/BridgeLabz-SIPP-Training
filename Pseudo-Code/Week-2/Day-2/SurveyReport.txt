Pseudo-code: Online Survey Report Generator

1. Define class SurveyResponse with fields: restaurantName, rating
2. Store list of responses
3. Group responses by restaurantName
4. For each restaurant, calculate average rating
5. Print restaurant → average rating
6. Identify:
   a. Restaurant with highest average
   b. Restaurant with lowest average
7. Example run:
   PizzaHut → 4.5
   KFC → 3.5
   Subway → 4.67
